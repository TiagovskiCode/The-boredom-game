# 🎮 The Boredom Game

### One phone. Your friends. No more "what should we do?"

A social party-game hub made for friends to play together in person. Add
your players, choose a game or let the roulette decide, and keep track
of the night's score --- all from one device.

[**▶ Play The Boredom
Game**](https://tiagovskicode.github.io/The-boredom-game/)
:::✨ About the Project
The Boredom Game is a browser-based collection of social games
designed to bring people together around one phone. It was created by
Tiago Teixeira and Gabriel with a simple goal: make it easier for a
group of friends to start playing instead of reaching for their own
phones.

No account, installation, or complicated setup is needed. Open the
website, add the people playing, and get started.
🚀 Features
- Player management --- add or remove players as your group
  changes.
- Game catalogue --- browse available games in one place.
- Search and filters --- find games by name or category, including
  Party, Challenge, Quiz, Duel, and Utilities.
- Game roulette --- let the roulette choose a game that fits the
  current number of players.
- Session scoreboard --- keep track of points across the night's
  games.
- Favourites and recommendations --- make it easier to find games
  you like.
- Browser-based progress --- player names, scores, and favourites
  are saved in the browser so the session can continue after a
  refresh.
- No account required --- play directly from the website.
- Designed for shared play --- pass one phone around the group and
  play together.
Some visual elements, fonts, animations, and 3D effects may require an
internet connection.

🕹️ How to Play
1. Open The Boredom
   Game.
2. Add the names of everyone playing.
3. Browse the game catalogue and choose a game, or use the roulette for
   a random pick.
4. Follow the selected game's instructions and keep the session
   scoreboard updated.
5. Pass the phone to the next player and keep the fun going.
